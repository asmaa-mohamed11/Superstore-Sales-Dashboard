# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard

An interactive sales dashboard built with **Microsoft Excel** as part of the **Elevvo Pathways Internship**. The goal was to turn raw sales data into clear business insights that support decision-making.

## 📸 Preview


![Dashboard](images/dashboard.png)



## 📈 Key Metrics
| KPI | Value |
|-----|-------|
| Total Sales | $2.26M |
| Total Orders | 4,922 |
| Average Order Value | $459.48 |
| Total Customers | 793 |

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Technology** was the top category with **$827K** in sales, driven mainly by the Phones sub-category
- **West** was the top region with **$710K**, followed by East ($670K)
- **Consumer** segment made up the largest share of sales at **51%**
- Monthly trend shows periods of higher and lower performance over time
- Top product: Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier (~$62K)

## 📊 Dashboard Components
- KPI cards
- Total Sales by Category
- Total Sales by Region
- Total Sales by Segment
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top 10 Products by Sales

## 🛠️ Tools
- Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, PivotCharts, Dashboard design)
